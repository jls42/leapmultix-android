LeapMultix helps children aged 6 to 12 master the four operations: multiplication, addition, subtraction and division. Pick the operation on the home screen, then learn your own way.

SIX GAME MODES
• Discovery: each table shown as dots, jumps or counting, with its trick.
• Quiz: multiple-choice questions, with the calculation explained after each mistake.
• Challenge: a race against the clock.
• Adventure: ten levels to open one after the other, earning stars.
• Chrono: ten right answers as fast as you can; missed calculations go to a review list.
• Arcade: six mini-games.

SIX ARCADE MINI-GAMES
MultiInvaders, MultiMiam, MultiMemory, MultiSnake, MultiPop and MultiGoal. The question is the same, shown at the top of the screen; the move changes with each game: aim, run through a maze, find a pair, steer a snake, tap the right spot, shoot at the goal.

FOR CHILDREN
• Five characters to play: the fox, the panda, the unicorn, the dragon and the astronaut, some of them unlocked with coins earned by playing.
• A recorded voice goes with the game, in English, French and Spanish.
• Light and dark colour themes, and a festive look for Halloween, Christmas and New Year.
• Calculations that cause trouble come back more often.

FOR PARENTS AND TEACHERS
• One profile per child on the same device.
• A dashboard: games played, records, tables to review.
• Adjustable text size and high contrast.
• Plays in portrait and in landscape.

NO ADS, NO ACCOUNT, NO CONNECTION
• No ads, no purchases, no account.
• The app collects no data: profiles and scores stay on the device.
• The whole game is in the app: it works without Internet.
